    04 ⎯ In use

    Say it plainly

    Open apertures keep every letter distinct, from a street sign to the smallest line of an interface. Nothing closes in on itself, so words stay clear at a glance and at a distance.

    The construction is strict; the result is not. Soft geometry gives text a warm, even tone.

    Made for the everyday

    Plainspoken is at home in wayfinding, interfaces, reports and running text, anywhere clarity matters more than decoration.

    Æble, Øresund, Kraków, Brno, İzmir, Dvořák. Extended Latin support covers most European languages.

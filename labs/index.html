Labs. Tools and experiments with no brief, made because they were fun to make.

01 · Particle Lab

Quarks

Quarks gathers type and shapes into grains of light in depth. Drop in a font or an image, choose how the particles move and play. Save a frame or record a take.

Open Quarks →

02 · Glyph Lab

U+

Every character worth knowing, one click from the clipboard: arrows, quotes, spaces, math, shapes and more, with names and codes. Drop in a font to see what it covers.

Open U+ →

03 · Grade Lab

ΔE

Colour grades from directors, series, films, photographers and film stocks, turned into image-edit prompts for Nano Banana, GPT Image and FLUX. The browser shows a rough sketch of the grade; the prompt carries the real look.

Open ΔE →

04 · Type Lab

Ductus

How a glyph is built: outline, points, handles, metrics and a construction layer fitted to the real contour. Open any font file; everything runs on your own device.

Open Ductus →

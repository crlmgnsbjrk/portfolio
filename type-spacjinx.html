Space is overrated. Presence isn’t.

Meet Spacjinx. A bold, unapologetic typeface that takes up space and makes every bit of it count. Wide proportions, tight spacing and a whole lot of confidence. Built for big statements, strong identities and brands that refuse to whisper.

    04 ⎯ In use

    Made to be seen

    Spacjinx is at home where type has a job to do from far away: posters, logotypes, packaging, signage and the first line of a campaign. Wide letters fill the format; tight spacing keeps a word together as one strong shape.

    Set it big and let it breathe on its own terms. It does not need much around it.

    Seven ways to be loud

    From Light to Black, every style keeps the same wide stance. The lighter weights bring a calm, architectural feel; the heavy ones are pure attitude.

    Æble, Øresund, Kraków, Brno, İzmir, Dvořák. Extended Latin support covers most European languages.

    04 ⎯ In use

    Circles, lines and common sense

    Geometry gives Seamless its calm: round bowls, straight stems and even proportions that make a page feel ordered before a single word is read.

    Then the edges are softened by eye, not by compass, so text stays friendly and readable at every size.

    Five styles, one voice

    Light sets the quiet, larger moments; Regular and Medium carry running text, interfaces and the everyday; SemiBold and Bold take the headlines, the signs and the statements.

    Æble, Øresund, Kraków, Brno, İzmir, Dvořák. Extended Latin support covers most European languages.

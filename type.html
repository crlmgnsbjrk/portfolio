    04 ⎯ In use

    Grid, order and a little warmth

    Swiss typography taught us that clarity is a form of generosity. A good typeface gets out of the way, lets the content speak and holds a page together without asking for attention.

    Almost Swiss keeps that discipline, then loosens the tie a little. Open apertures and calm proportions keep text even at small sizes, while a few details give it a voice of its own.

    From caption to headline

    Nine styles from Thin to Black cover everything from fine print to posters. The lighter weights are made for large sizes and quiet confidence; the heavier ones for signage, packaging and anything that needs to be seen from across a room.

    Æble, Øresund, Kraków, Brno, İzmir, Dvořák. Extended Latin support covers most European languages.

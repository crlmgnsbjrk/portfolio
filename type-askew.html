Balance is overrated. Character isn’t.

Meet Askew. An asymmetric sans that leans into the unexpected. Shifted weights, uneven curves and off-centre details give every letter a rhythm of its own. Familiar enough to read, curious enough to remember. Built for brands and voices that would rather be interesting than correct.

    04 ⎯ In use

    Off-centre on purpose

    Most typefaces hide their tensions; Askew shows them. Bowls sit a little to one side, strokes thin where you do not expect them, and the page picks up a quiet, uneven pulse.

    Set in a line of text it still reads as a calm sans. Up close, every letter has an opinion.

    Five styles, light on its feet

    From ExtraLight to SemiBold, Askew stays conversational rather than loud. It works for editorial, identities and interfaces that want a voice without raising it.

    Æble, Øresund, Kraków, Brno, İzmir, Dvořák. Extended Latin support covers most European languages.

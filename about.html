Carl Magnus Björk, Creative Director and designer in Stockholm.

I’m a Creative Director and multidisciplinary brand designer with 15+ years of experience, currently at Greenely. Previously, I spent five years at Northvolt as a Senior Brand Designer.

I focus on visual identities, brand systems and art direction across digital and physical touchpoints.

From award-winning global campaigns to scalable startup identities, I bring a calm, collaborative and hands-on approach. I always aim for the core of the idea and the business behind it, then flip it just enough to stand out.
